01 · Subjects & shared state

Who gets to hear the stream?

A Subject lets you send values to many listeners. Its different flavors decide whether a late listener hears only what happens next — or gets some history too.

In plain language

A Subject is like a live group chat.

People who are listening now hear new messages. If someone joins late, a regular Subject doesn’t replay old messages. Choose a replaying Subject when a new listener needs the latest state or a little history.

Real-world example

Think of a live dashboard

A new chart may need the current temperature immediately, while an activity feed may want the last few events.

What to notice

Subject forgets; BehaviorSubject immediately gives its current value; ReplaySubject replays its configured history; AsyncSubject waits for completion and then gives the final value.
